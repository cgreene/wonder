# Market summary — gen-AI-for-work TAM

Research for the wonder / OhWow deck. Scope: **global**, people **actively using
generative-AI assistants for work** (ChatGPT, Claude, Microsoft 365 Copilot,
Gemini, GitHub Copilot), plus the **$ gen-AI software market**. Data current through
~Q1 2026. Compiled 2026-06-04.

## Headline numbers (slide-ready)

| Metric | Figure | Growth | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **People TAM** — global, gen-AI *for work* (2024–25) | **~300M** (range 250–350M) | **~25–35% CAGR** → ~1B+ by 2030 | Medium (triangulated estimate) |
| **$ market** — global gen-AI software (2025) | **~$40–63B** | **~29–37% CAGR** → ~$220B by 2030 | High |
| **Hard floor** — paid work seats today | **~27M+** | Copilot +250% YoY | High |

> The 300M people figure is a **triangulated estimate**, not a single published
> number — label it as such on any slide.

## People TAM — how we reach ~300M

No vendor or analyst publishes a clean "people using gen-AI for work" headcount, so
it is triangulated between a hard floor and a survey-based ceiling:

- **Floor (disclosed paid seats):** Microsoft 365 Copilot **20M paid seats**
  (FY26 Q3, +250% YoY) + OpenAI ChatGPT for Work **7M seats / 1M+ business orgs**
  (Nov 2025) ≈ **~27M paid seats** — before Gemini, GitHub Copilot, Claude, and the
  far larger *free* work-usage population. [MS earnings][ms-q3], [OpenAI][oai].
- **Ceiling:** ~**1.25B** global information workers (Forrester, 2018) — larger now,
  but **not** the TAM (much of the global workforce is informal/subsistence and not a
  SaaS market per [ILO][ilo]). [Forrester][forrester].
- **Penetration → headline:** active work use is a **minority** behavior. Microsoft's
  "75% of knowledge workers use AI" is self-reported *any* use; actual active/daily
  use is far lower — PwC **54% past-year but only 14% daily**, Pew & Brookings
  **~21% of US workers**. Applying ~20–30% active use to the ~1.2–1.3B knowledge-worker
  base → **~250–400M**, central **~300M**. [Microsoft WTI][wti], [PwC][pwc], [Pew][pew], [Brookings][brookings].
- **CAGR:** from observed adoption (Pew 16%→21% in one year; Copilot seats +250% YoY),
  decelerating as the market matures → **~25–35%**.

## $ market — ~$40–63B → ~$220B

- **Anchor:** ABI Research — **$63B (2025) → ~$220B (2030), 28.9% CAGR**. [ABI][abi].
- **Corroborating bookend:** Precedence Research — **$37.89B (2025), ~37% CAGR**
  (→ ~$1.2T by 2035 on the higher-growth view). [Precedence][prec].
- **Caveat:** estimates vary **3×+** across firms for the *same* 2025 market
  (Grand View ~$22B, GMI ~$54B, M&M ~$71B). Cite ABI as the anchor with a range;
  avoid false precision (say "~$220B").

## OhWow's own market (the number for the deck)

The figures above are the **adoption TAM** — the wave OhWow rides, *not* the revenue it
captures (OhWow sells researcher connection, not gen-AI). OhWow's own market is bottoms-up
and grows in rings (the "Facebook started on one campus" arc):

- **Beachhead — researchers (free).** ~**9M** researchers run R&D worldwide (UNESCO/UIS:
  ~8.8M FTE researchers, 2018; ~14.3M incl. all R&D personnel) — the signal and the network. [UNESCO][unesco].
- **Revenue now (SAM).** Institutions pay **$100 / researcher / yr** → **~$1B/yr** at ~9M
  researchers. Sensitivity: $50 → ~$0.5B, $150 → ~$1.4B.
- **Follow-on.** Connections graduate from Discord rooms to **real-world venues / events** —
  revenue where the network meets in person.
- **The prize (expansion).** As the wedge widens from researchers → everyone using AI for work
  (~300M today → ~1B+ by 2030, per the TAM above), the verified, high-intent network becomes
  **an advertising platform** (opt-in, aligned — *not* surveillance). At a ~$100 blended
  seat/ARPU that expansion is **tens of $B** (~$30B+ at 300M).

> Tension to own: the advertising thesis cuts against the product's privacy-first promise.
> Frame ads as opt-in, aligned offers (tools, grants, roles) — never resale of the raw signal.

## Honesty caveats (defend the slide)

1. **"Uses gen-AI for work" ≠ "has touched gen-AI."** Active/daily work use ~14–21%,
   not the 54–75% "any use" headlines.
2. **Platform user counts mislead.** ChatGPT ~800M weekly users (~10% of world adults),
   but **>70% of messages are non-work and that share is growing (53%→73%)** — don't
   equate ChatGPT users with work users. [NBER w34255][nber].
3. **Seats ≠ people** (licenses can overcount); **1M+ OpenAI "business customers" =
   organizations, not people** — keep those lines distinct.
4. Surveys are **self-reported and often US-only** — don't extrapolate the US 21% globally.

## Suggested slide framing

A **bounded funnel** beats a single number, and survives a skeptic:

- **Ceiling** ~1.25B knowledge workers →
- **TAM** ~300M actively using gen-AI for work today (~25–35% CAGR → ~1B by 2030) →
- **Anchored by** ~27M+ hard paid seats today,
- alongside the **$ market** (~$60B today → ~$220B by 2030).

The floor proves the market is real; the growth rate sells the trajectory.
